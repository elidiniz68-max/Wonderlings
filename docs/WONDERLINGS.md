# Wonderlings — Creature System (MVP Spec)

**Purpose.** The 5 MVP Wonderlings in full: personalities, talents, rarity,
evolutions, unlock methods, animations — plus the system rules everything
future (worlds, events) builds on.

**System principles (locked).**
1. **Companions, not power-ups.** A Wonderling never makes a child *better
   at learning* — no creature gates progress, boosts XP, or is "best."
   Rarity = prestige and flair, never power. A struggling learner's Pyro is
   exactly as good as a thriving learner's.
2. **Milo teaches; Wonderlings befriend.** Only Milo gives instruction,
   hints, and praise-words. Wonderlings *react* (cheer, comfort, show off).
   The learning voice stays single and consistent.
3. **No loot boxes. Ever.** Eggs are earned by named achievements, never
   bought, never random-paid. (Apple/Google kids' policies + parent trust.
   This is a brand pillar, not a placeholder.)
4. **Every creature is earnable through play.** No creature is paywalled.
   Monetization (step 9/10) touches cosmetics and Plus — never creatures.
5. **Evolutions reward journey, not skill.** Triggers are counts and
   milestones (challenges attempted, worlds restored, levels reached) —
   never accuracy thresholds. A child who gets 40% right still evolves.

## Rarity tiers

| Tier | Meaning | MVP members |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Your first friends; world eggs | Pyro, Sylva |
| **Rare** | Named achievements | Bubbles, Pebbles |
| **Epic** | Big milestones | Sparkle |
| **Legendary** | Post-MVP: events, seasons, secrets | (step: none in MVP) |

Rarity changes: visual flair (particle effects, name shimmer), collection
card border, and bragging rights. Nothing else.

## Talents (what they DO)

Talents are small, transparent, non-divisive perks — flavor + convenience,
never learning advantage. Anything touching coins/hints is flagged for
step 8 (economy) review.

| Creature | Talent | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Pyro | **Victory Spin** | Signature celebration on mission complete (flair) |
| Sylva | **Word of the Day** | Shares one kid-safe vocab word on the Collection screen (flavor + gentle learning, not instruction) |
| Bubbles | **Treasure Dive** | After each mission, finds +5 bonus WonderCoins (fixed, transparent — ⚠️ economy review) |
| Pebbles | **Second Chance** | Once per mission, after a wrong answer, offers a retry of the SAME question (kindness mechanic — ⚠️ economy review) |
| Sparkle | **Spotlight** | Special star-shower on level-up (flair) |

## The five — full sheets

### 🔥 PYRO — Fire · Number Nebula egg · Starter
- **Personality:** bold, impulsive, big-hearted. Acts first, cheers loudest,
  cries (happy tears, obviously) at your victories.
- **Voice style:** exclamations. *"LET'S GOOO!"*
- **Home behaviors:** loop-de-loop when you start a streak; naps curled
  beside the reactor; sneezes tiny harmless embers when excited.
- **Mission reactions:** correct → happy hop; wrong → headbutt of
  encouragement (gentle!); streak → loop-de-loop.
- **Talent:** Victory Spin — the mission-complete spin. Nobody spins like Pyro.
- **Evolutions:** Pyro → **🐉 Pyron** (50 math challenges — grows little
  wings, sleeker flame crest) → **🔥🐲 INFERION** (150 math challenges —
  majestic, ember trail, name in ALL CAPS).
- **Animations:** idle flicker, happy hop, celebrate spin, sleep (ember dims
  to a glow).

### 🌿 SYLVA — Leaf · Storywood egg · Starter
- **Personality:** curious, gentle, wise beyond her size. Asks about words.
  Collects fallen "word petals."
- **Voice style:** soft wonder. *"Ooooh… what does that word mean?"*
- **Home behaviors:** sways like leaves in wind; tilts head when Milo
  explains something; waters a tiny sprout that grows per mission you finish.
- **Mission reactions:** correct → leaf-twirl; wrong → leans in close,
  comforting; streak → petals burst.
- **Talent:** Word of the Day — one vocab gem on the Collection screen.
- **Evolutions:** Sylva → **🌿 Sylvana** (50 reading challenges — taller,
  vine patterns) → **🌳✨ ELDERWOOD** (150 reading challenges — blossom
  crown, ancient and kind).
- **Animations:** idle sway, happy twirl, celebrate petal-burst, sleep
  (curls like a closed bud).

### 🫧 BUBBLES — Water · Twin Worlds egg · Rare
*Unlock: restore BOTH Number Nebula and Storywood.*
- **Personality:** playful, loyal, goofy. Blows bubble rings shaped like
  stars when happy. Would follow you anywhere.
- **Voice style:** giggly. *"Hehe! Again, again!"*
- **Home behaviors:** bounces instead of walking; naps inside a big bubble;
  pops (harmlessly, reforming instantly) when surprised.
- **Mission reactions:** correct → bubble-ring blow; wrong → splashes a
  tiny comforting wave; streak → bubble fireworks.
- **Talent:** Treasure Dive — +5 WonderCoins after each mission, presented
  as "Bubbles found this for you!" (⚠️ step 8 review).
- **Evolutions:** Bubbles → **🌊 Bubblo** (100 total challenges — bigger,
  wave crest) → **💧🐋 TIDALORE** (post-MVP: restore 4 worlds — gentle
  whale-like majesty).
- **Animations:** idle bob, happy bounce, celebrate bubble-fireworks, sleep
  (floating in bubble).

### 🪨 PEBBLES — Stone · Scholar egg · Rare
*Unlock: 50 correct challenges (any subject).*
- **Personality:** steady, patient, deadpan funny. The calm one. Thinks
  before speaking; when Pebbles talks, everyone listens.
- **Voice style:** dry. *"Hmm. Steady."*
- **Home behaviors:** sits perfectly still for hours (it's a rock thing);
  collects shiny pebbles; slow-blinks at excitement.
- **Mission reactions:** correct → satisfied nod (a big deal for Pebbles);
  wrong → steadying rumble, "again"; streak → rare tiny smile.
- **Talent:** Second Chance — once per mission, retry the same question
  after a wrong answer. Same XP either way. Kindness, not advantage
  (⚠️ step 8 review).
- **Evolutions:** Pebbles → **🗿 Boulder** (100 correct answers — moss
  spreads, kinder eyes) → **⛰️ MOUNTAINHEART** (300 correct — towering,
  gentle, lichen constellations).
- **Animations:** idle stillness (breathing dust), happy nod, celebrate
  pebble-toss, sleep (indistinguishable from a rock — that's the joke).

### ✨ SPARKLE — Star · Explorer egg · Epic
*Unlock: reach Level 5.*
- **Personality:** bright, encouraging, a little dramatic. Lives for
  milestones. Your personal hype-star.
- **Voice style:** theatrical. *"Dahling, you LEVELED UP!"*
- **Home behaviors:** orbits your avatar slowly; dims politely during story
  moments; does a twirl when you open the app ("Welcome back, Explorer!").
- **Mission reactions:** correct → twinkle burst; wrong → soft glow pulse
  ("shine on!"); streak → constellation trail.
- **Talent:** Spotlight — level-ups get a full star-shower, not just the
  standard one.
- **Evolutions:** Sparkle → **🌟 Twinkle** (Level 10 — sharper points,
  comet tail) → **💫 NOVA PRIME** (Level 20 — radiant, slow orbit of
  mini-stars).
- **Animations:** idle twinkle, happy burst, celebrate star-shower, sleep
  (dims to a nightlight glow — doubles as a sleep cue. You're welcome,
  parents.).

## Evolution system rules

- **Trigger types:** challenge counts (any accuracy), worlds restored,
  levels reached. Never accuracy-gated, never paid, never random.
- **The cinematic:** 2-second glow-up — creature rises, light swirls, new
  form lands with name reveal + trait line. Skippable after first viewing.
- **Form persistence:** the Collection shows all unlocked stages; the child
  picks which stage appears on Home (some kids love the baby form — let them).
- **Evolution is announced, not sprung:** at 80% progress, Milo teases it
  ("Pyro's looking… different today. 10 more math challenges!").

## Bond system (MVP-light)

- Set ONE active companion (Home + mission corner). Others live happily in
  the Collection.
- Bond grows 1 ♥ per mission completed together. Purely expressive:
  - **Bond 1:** idle + happy reactions.
  - **Bond 2:** new idle variation + signature streak move.
  - **Bond 3:** golden nameplate + special celebration.
- Switching companions is free and guilt-free ("Pyro will cheer for you
  from the Collection!"). No decay, no punishment for swapping.

## Companions in missions

- The active companion sits in the mission corner (small, non-distracting).
- Reacts to events: correct (happy), wrong (comfort), streak (signature
  move), mission complete (joins the celebration).
- Never speaks instruction. Never blocks the question. Tappable for a
  happy bounce (because kids will tap it).
